Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 3, 2026

Amazon Product Filter ("the Extension") is a Chrome browser extension that filters and sorts Amazon search results on the client side. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the Extension accesses, how it is used, and what is stored.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

The Extension operates entirely within your browser. No analytics, tracking pixels, or telemetry of any kind are used.

2. Data Stored Locally

The Extension uses chrome.storage.local to save your filter and sort preferences (e.g., minimum rating, minimum review count, toggle states). This data:

  • Is stored only on your device
  • Never leaves your browser
  • Can be cleared by uninstalling the Extension or clearing browser data

3. Google Sign-In

The Extension offers an optional Google Sign-In to unlock advanced sorting features (Magic Score, Price sorting). When you sign in:

  • Authentication is handled entirely by Google's OAuth 2.0 flow via chrome.identity APIs
  • The Extension requests access only to your email address and basic profile name
  • This information is used solely to display your signed-in status within the Extension popup
  • No credentials, tokens, or profile data are sent to any external server controlled by us
  • You can sign out at any time, which revokes the token

4. Amazon Page Access

The Extension runs a content script on Amazon pages (amazon.com, amazon.in, amazon.co.uk, amazon.de, amazon.ca) to:

  • Read product listing data visible on the page (ratings, review counts, prices, badges)
  • Hide or reorder product cards based on your filter/sort settings

This processing happens entirely in your browser. No page content or browsing data is transmitted externally.

5. Permissions Used

  • storage — Save your filter/sort preferences locally
  • activeTab — Apply filters to the currently active Amazon tab
  • identity — Enable optional Google Sign-In for advanced features

6. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used is Google OAuth 2.0 for optional sign-in. No other third-party services, APIs, or SDKs are integrated. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising services.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any user data to any third party. Period.

8. Children's Privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
